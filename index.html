3 · Live

Connect PopQuiz to Claude

Your MCP link:

  1. In claude.ai, open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it PopQuiz, paste the MCP link above, and click Add. No sign-in needed.
  3. In a new chat, make sure PopQuiz is switched on in the tools menu, then ask: “Use PopQuiz to make a 5-question quiz on our leave policy.”
  4. Open the edit link Claude gives you. Log in with demo / popquiz2026.

What Claude can do with PopQuiz

create_quizPublish a quiz and get a share link + edit link
list_quizzesSee every quiz and how many people took it
get_quizRead the latest version, including your manual edits
update_quizRewrite questions, change pass mark, open or close
get_quiz_resultsScores and per-question accuracy, so Claude can spot weak topics
